# Onboard a customer and stand up their contract

Base URL: `https://integrators.prod.api.tabsplatform.com`. Authenticate every
call with the merchant API key: `Authorization: <API_KEY>` (generated by an
admin under Developers in the Tabs app).

1. **Create the customer** — `IntegratorsApiCustomersController_createCustomer`
   (`POST /v3/customers`). Provide `name`, `defaultCurrency` (defaults to USD),
   and the primary billing contact. Capture the returned customer `id` (UUID).
   For child billing entities use
   `IntegratorsApiCustomersController_createSubCustomer` with a
   `parentCustomerId`.
2. **Attach contacts and address** —
   `IntegratorsApiCustomersController_createContacts` and
   `IntegratorsApiCustomersController_createAddress` for billing/shipping
   details used on invoices.
3. **Create the contract** —
   `IntegratorsApiContractsv3Controller_createContract`
   (`POST /v3/contracts`), linked to the customer. Alternatively upload the
   executed agreement with
   `IntegratorsApiContractsv3Controller_uploadContractFile` and let Tabs AI
   extract terms; each contract belongs to exactly one customer.
4. **Add obligations** — `IntegratorsApiContractsv3Controller_createObligation`
   for each commitment. Obligations reference a GL Item and generate the
   invoice line items.
5. **Add billing terms** —
   `IntegratorsApiContractsv3Controller_createBillingTerm` to control cadence
   and amounts; group behavior is managed via billing term groups.
